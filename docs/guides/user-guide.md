# User Guide

## Recruiters

1. **Requisitions:** create one (or receive one from a hiring manager) and click *Save & submit*. The Requisition
   agent flags missing information, budget issues and exclusionary wording before approval.
2. **Jobs:** once approved, *Create job*. The JD agent drafts the description. Review requirements (mark must-haves
   as mandatory, set weights), add knock-out questions, then *Publish*. The job appears on your careers site and the
   XML job-board feed.
3. **Screening:** new applications are parsed and screened automatically, then land in **AI screening**. Open each
   one:
   - *Facts* are extracted from the CV and quote the source line.
   - *Rule checks* show exactly which requirements passed.
   - *Interpretation* is AI-generated (or rule-based) and advisory.
   - Choose *Advance* (to assessment or interview), *Reject* or *Hold*. If you disagree with the AI, add a reason.
4. **Assessments:** invite from the candidate workspace. Objective answers are scored automatically; confirm scores
   for free-text answers.
5. **Interviews:** pick the panel, click *Suggest slots*, then choose one. Invitations and calendar events go out
   automatically, and reminders are sent 24 hours before.
6. **Verification and offers:** request references and background checks (with consent). When complete, *Draft
   offer*, adjust if needed, submit for approval, then *Send*.
7. **Onboarding:** after acceptance, *Hand off to HRIS / onboarding* creates the checklist.

## Hiring managers

Approve requisitions, interview, and make the evaluation decision in the candidate workspace. The Evaluation agent
compiles evidence and flags interviewer disagreement or missing feedback; you decide and record a rationale.

## Interviewers

Open the interview from your calendar. Use the structured guide, optionally paste a transcript (with candidate
consent) for an AI summary, and submit your scorecard independently. You won't see others' scores first.

## Finance / approvers

Approve out-of-band offers and budgets from notifications or the Offers page.

## Candidates

Apply on the careers site. You choose whether AI may assist the screening; a person always decides. Track your
status, withdraw, download your data or request deletion in the candidate portal (sign in with an e-mailed link).
